Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25. The operator of this hosted service is the data controller for the data described below. (Self-hosting the open-source code yourself? Then you are the controller for your deployment.)

What we collect

  • Account identity (signed-in users): Google account id, email, and name via OAuth. Used only for sign-in, quota, and billing tier.
  • Your questions: the free-form questions you ask about apps, stored with your job records so answers can be retrieved and quotas counted.
  • Review cache: public Google Play reviews, with reviewer names and images dropped at ingest — never stored, never sent to the AI provider.
  • Usage records: token counts and cost estimates per analysis. Full prompts and review corpora are never written to logs.

International transfers

Reviews and questions are sent to the configured AI provider (Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint) to generate answers. Review data originating in the EU is therefore transferred to that provider when you request an analysis.

Retention

Finished or failed jobs and their linked usage records are purged after 90 days (configurable via DATA_RETENTION_DAYS). Expired review snapshots are reclaimed once no cached answer references them. The shared, de-identified review cache contains no account data.

Delete my data

Signed-in users can delete all of their owned data (jobs, usage records, and account row) at any time via DELETE /api/me/data. Anonymous trial sessions can delete their own browser's jobs the same way.

Scraping notice

This service scrapes publicly visible Play Store pages through an unofficial library, which Google may throttle or block at any time. The operator bears that operational risk: when live data is unavailable, answers are served from the cache and clearly labeled as such. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
